Put your ranch photos in this folder, using these file names
(or edit the paths in `src/App.jsx` at the top, in the IMG object,
to match whatever you name your own files):

  hero.jpg         wide pasture/landscape shot for the top banner
  barn.jpg         the main barn or farmhouse
  roundup.jpg      working cattle / horseback banner, "the ranch year"
  dusty-drive.jpg  the road/drive into the ranch
  beef-1.jpg       Angus cattle on pasture
  beef-2.jpg       breeding stock / bulls
  beef-3.jpg       a single cow, close up
  dairy-1.jpg      Jerseys in the milking barn
  dairy-2.jpg      dairy herd on pasture
  dairy-3.jpg      a single dairy cow, close up
  steak.jpg        beef product shot
  milk.jpg         milk product shot
  cows-2.jpg       extra cattle shot used on the stays section

Any photo you haven't added yet will just show a small
"Add photo: ..." placeholder on the site instead of breaking anything,
so you can fill these in one at a time.
